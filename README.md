# Task 19 – Top 10 Products Analysis

## Objective
To identify the top 10 products based on total sales using the Superstore Sales Dataset.

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets

## Work Performed
1. Identified unique product names.
2. Calculated total sales for each product using the SUMIF formula.
3. Compared product sales.
4. Identified the top 10 products based on total sales.
5. Created a bar chart to visualize the results.

## Key Findings
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier ranked first with total sales of 61,599.824.
- Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch Plastic Comb Binding Machine ranked second.
- Cisco TelePresence System EX90 Videoconferencing Unit ranked third.

## Outcome
Successfully identified the top 10 products with the highest total sales and presented the results using a chart in Google Sheets.
